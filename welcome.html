GForm to GPT
v4.6.0

Google Forms, answered by AI

Scan any Google Form, get answers from ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, and review them before they're filled in. One extension, three engines, no typing.

Open the extension icon and accept the terms to begin.

How it works

1

Open any Google Form

The extension panel appears on the page. Press Ctrl+Shift+M to toggle it.

2

Scan and pick an engine

Click Scan & Extract Questions. Your AI opens in a new tab with the questions ready.

3

Review, then fill

Paste the AI response, check each answer, then fill the form. Saved profile details fill themselves in.

What it does

Form analysis

Reads the live page structure to extract every question, option, and image, including radio and dropdown groups.

Three engines

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini through their normal web pages. No API keys needed.

Local by default

Scans, answers, and history stay in your browser. Anonymous usage data leaves only if you allow it.

Human-paced typing

Answers are typed at a natural pace. You can switch to instant filling anytime.

Get started

Open the extension popup and accept the terms to unlock every feature. The panel opens on any Google Form, or press Ctrl+Shift+M.
